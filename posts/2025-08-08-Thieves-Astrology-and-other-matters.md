## Friday, August 8

### Entry 1:

It’s 10:42 in the morning; there’s some drama going on—well, more than one thing. I only have 20 minutes before the next dynamic titled “Emotional Release RRTM.”

The first issue is more material: yesterday, when I was going to shower, I ran into a neighbour from a nearby tent. She looked very stressed because there was water inside her tent. I helped her a bit, and she asked me for a hammer. I borrowed one from a man who had one.

After a while, a friend of hers arrived, and since they no longer needed help, I left and told them where to return the hammer.

This morning, I went to check whether they had returned it to the man, and he told me no. After checking with the girl, she said her friend had returned it to another tent. Meanwhile, there was another girl in the neighbouring tent. I asked her, and she told me very casually she knew nothing about the hammer or the girl. I said, “How can that be, if she’s your tent neighbor?” And she replied, “She’s not my neighbor. This isn’t my tent—I’m here stealing.” I suppose she was joking.

The other issues are emotional in nature; they have to do with my difficulty allowing myself to be loved.

There’s a girl with whom, since the first day during the dynamics, we’ve connected and had a great time. She’s very beautiful, cheerful, lively—but I always look for flaws. Even though I barely know her, I think she’s a good person.

But I think she’s not my type of woman. Maybe it’s my fear of opening up to her and letting myself be loved. I’m afraid of someone getting attached to me because if, at some point, I decide I’m not comfortable, I wouldn’t know how to pull away without hurting her.

I need to talk to her to clarify things.

During yesterday’s dynamic, we paired up, and she surrendered to the exercise, opening her legs and sitting in front of me. She told me she has a good job, an apartment, and what she lacks is a girlfriend—someone to share her journey with. Could it be her? She lives in Granada and I live in Falmouth. I’d have to move to Granada and be closer to my parents. But then, when my parents die, I’d only have her—and I don’t know if that’s better than being alone. And what about being with myself and with her?

What if I’m the one who openly declares myself to a girl who truly is my type? Do I face the fear of rejection? Maybe someone closer, in Falmouth?

### Entry 2:

It’s 8:37 p.m., and I’ve just had a super interesting day. Since I don’t have much time, I’ll try to be brief.

This morning’s workshop—I don’t remember the name—was incredible. I think it achieved what it intended: helping me detach from the ego, that false self. Realising that everything around me is simply an interpretation of my mental state, based on the information received from the aggregates (sensory inputs + mental).

The practice began with all of us lying on the ground in a circle, in contact with the earth, visualizing a light energy descending from the sky—a ray or point of light—entering at the precise moment when the sperm fertilizes the egg, passing through the fetal stage, the process of leaving the womb, the first faces, the first sensations.

Suddenly, our power animal appeared. In my case, it was a brown bear, which later evolved into a lion.

Later, I saw a very tall tree and began venerating it as a representation of all the energy of the universe. I hugged it and climbed it like a sloth; then the image of a lion came to me, and my movements became calmer, more majestic.

I ended in human form, standing shirtless and without glasses in front of the sun, symbolizing the clear light—which is the essence of all sentient beings.

It was interesting that when I lowered my head, everything became dark, but I happened to be in a place where the tree shadows blocked the light. I played with that for a while and then chose to remain calm, illuminated by the sun.

To finish, with eyes half-open, we walked around and encountered the other beings, recognising each one by their pure and perfect nature of clear light. There were moments when we crossed gazes and embraced.

At one point, I bumped into a massage therapist girl who, later, at the pool, thanked me and explained her journey. She said she had been trapped in a vortex—what the Kabbalah calls “the wheel of fortune”—and when she collided with me, I helped her break out of that spiral. Afterwards, she told me she went to lean against a tree.

To conclude the workshop, we all sat in a circle and expressed our experiences. I said:

“Thank you all because I saw in each one of you beings of light—including myself—and I could identify myself in each of you. It makes me think that perhaps in parallel universes, I am living all my reincarnations at once. I could see myself in other existences: as a woman, as a man, younger, older, uglier, more handsome. All clear light in different containers that are merely interpretations of my mind.”

There’s much more to explain.

Right now, I sense that people here are starting to get tired, and there’s a slight hostile atmosphere—but only among certain individuals (at least in my perception). Small groups and couples are forming.

After the workshop, I spoke with the French musician about trees and the magical nature of this place. He told me to walk down a corridor of trees and see what energy I found.

At the end of that path, there was a girl whose name I don’t remember. We talked a lot. Very beautiful. I told her very intimate and private things, but in a very natural way. Honestly, I think it was the best conversation I’ve had in a long time. She’s from Sant Celoni and has the same name as the girl I bumped into during the workshop—what a coincidence.

The afternoon workshop didn’t appeal to me at all. I tried one with a Japanese woman who kept making us act silly to relax and sing, without understanding the purpose of the practice called “ToGo.” I left and ran into another woman who felt the same. We sat down for tea with a craftswoman who was weaving dreamcatchers.

Later, the astrologer—“the guy from Seville”—joined us. Talking about his life, topics of esotericism and occult sciences came up, and I shared an experience known only to a few: the Ouija board.

28CC. From 28 to 29. The “Original Camino de Santiago.” It changed my life. Death of the old continuous mental stream and beginning of the present continuous mental state. Or rather, a change of direction—thanks to which I am here now.

He also did my birth chart and, from what little we had shared, guessed my zodiac sign. He told me I have the moon in Libra, meaning I seek balance, I’m tender and loving, I dislike conflict and try to avoid it, and I’m very indecisive. 100% accurate.

My neck and upper back hurt a bit from “being an animal” so intensely—I was doing uncontrolled somersaults. As I said, I think I’ve had enough workshops. I’ll try, but if it’s not for me, I’ll leave.

I also bought nettle soap bars. On the way, at one of the stalls, I ran into one of the pioneers of the Camino, who gave me good advice.

Tonight there’s “El Bhuio.” I’ll check it out, but I’m going in total chill mode.

### Entry 3:

It’s 10:46 p.m. I’ve just returned to the tent after dinner.

While I was first in line, the organisers came to the outdoor dining area. After exchanging glances, I dared to say a few words of gratitude. However, next to me there was a girl who seemed very angry. She was not only pointing out things that could be improved but reproaching them for not offering solutions. When one of them told her to focus on the positive, that made her even angrier. (Although she has a point, I think as the organizer of an event like this, you could show more tact in such situations, since some people don’t respond to reason.)

At first, I didn’t want to eat much, but once I was there, I ended up having dinner.

During dinner, I sat opposite a man named Antonio, who told me about similar festivals and about his time on the beaches of Cádiz—but the most interesting part comes now.

A girl whose name I don’t know invited me to write. I told her about this morning’s experience, and she said the bear symbolises wisdom and that I have come here to leave a mark.

During the conversation, I remembered something I hadn’t written earlier.

During the open-eyed meditation, I focused on the sky, and in the distance, I saw subtle white lines forming hexagonal tiles. Then those lines began to move, forming star-like patterns, like a mosaic of blue tiles with white lines.

Then my mind grasped the idea that I was inside a dome, like in the movie The Truman Show.
